# ResignationDashBoard
Power BI Employee Resignation Dashboard analyzing attrition trends across departments, education levels, gender, and stock option levels. Features interactive visualizations and gauge charts to evaluate work-life balance, job satisfaction, education, and stock option metrics, providing actionable workforce insights for HR decision-making.

### KPI Questions for the Employee Resignation Dashboard

1. What is the total number of employee resignations?
2. What is the employee resignation rate (%)?
3. Which department records the highest resignation rate?
4. Which department has the lowest resignation rate?
5. How does stock option level affect employee resignations?
6. How does employee education level impact resignation trends?
7. What is the resignation distribution by gender?
8. What is the average job satisfaction score?
9. Does lower job satisfaction correlate with higher resignations?
10. How does work-life balance influence employee retention?

### Processes Followed to Develop the Employee Resignation Dashboard
   * Identified the goal of analyzing employee attrition and its key drivers.
   * Determined the KPIs required to support HR decision-making.
   * Obtained employee data containing information on attrition, department, gender, education, stock option levels, job satisfaction, and work-life balance.
   * Removed duplicate records.
   * Handled missing or inconsistent values.
   * Corrected data types and standardized categorical fields.
   * Ensured data quality for accurate reporting.
   * Used Power Query to transform and organize the dataset.
   * Created meaningful categories and prepared data for analysis.
   
   * Developed measures for:

     * Total Employees
     * Total Resignations
     * Rate of Resignation
     * Total Employees (Current)

   * Created visualizations to analyze Resignation by:

     * Department
     * Gender
     * Education Level
     * Stock Option Level
    
   * Incorporated gauge charts to monitor:

     * Work-Life Balance
     * Job Satisfaction
     * Education
     * Stock Option Levels
       
   * Added slicers and filters for dynamic analysis.
   * Enabled cross-filtering across visuals for deeper insights.
   * Tested user interactions and dashboard responsiveness.
   * Analyzed trends and patterns affecting employee resignations.

### Outcome

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of employee resignation, enabling HR teams and management to identify resignation trends, assess workforce satisfaction, evaluate retention factors, and make data-driven decisions to improve employee engagement.
